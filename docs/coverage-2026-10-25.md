# MX ASTRO – scoped calculation coverage, 2026-10-25

This is a **source inspection ledger**, not a claim that every body, chart pair, time or screen has been verified. Project: `mazenmix/orbit` branch `audit/2026-10-25-engine-consistency`. No production deployment.

## Source contracts
- Swiss Ephemeris WASM via `src/engine.ts`; civil times via Temporal/IANA historical zones. Tropical ecliptic longitude and longitudinal speed; Placidus houses.
- `src/math.ts`: 5 longitude aspects (0°,60°,90°,120°,180°), with per-transit orb limits. The natal transit calculator includes only explicitly listed, eligible transit bodies and natal targets.
- `src/wholeSkyBroadcast.ts`: 10 major bodies, pairs, 5 angles, ingress and longitude-speed stations; a **bounded hourly survey**, not proof against every multiple/tangent crossing or unmodelled aspect.
- `src/asteroidRuntime.ts` and `src/asteroids.ts`: 56 separately named MPC asteroids; calculations require mounted, validated SE1 binary data. Do **not** confuse numbered asteroid Lilith (1181) with a derived lunar-apogee point.
- `src/engine.ts` `compatibility()`: 10 native planets plus eligible natal angles; the 6 built-in minor bodies and 56 optional ones are not part of the core two-person relation pairs.
- `src/main.ts` is a multi-screen consumer; consumer parity, live browser text, saved private profiles and actual deployed build are not certified by this ledger.

## Coverage totals from declarations
- 10/10 declared major bodies in the shared **whole-sky event** scanner; no minor or catalogue asteroid in this scanner.
- 16/16 declared built-in body IDs in `math.ts`; 10 always used in base natal/synastry, 6 conditional when minor-body calculation is requested.
- 56/56 defined named MPC asteroid identifiers; **not** the same as 56 verified, currently loaded positions. Availability can vary by binary and requested epoch.
- 2 calculated chart axes (ASC, MC), 12 house cusps conditional on time/location/Placidus validity.
- 5/5 configured primary longitude aspect types in core; unimplemented or unregistered secondary/declination aspects must not be reported as calculated.
- Derived Mean/True Lunar Node, Vertex, additional chart angles, declination-aspect support, and fixed-star interpretation: **not present in this inspected core registry**; other uninspected modules may have separate logic. No synthetic output authorized.

## Observed cross-consumer gaps / status
- Whole-sky broadcast != all 72 supported/optional objects. It uses 10 by design.
- Natal targets in `report()` use the base `chart()` (10 bodies + conditional axes), while transits use the 16-body request. This means a minor body can act as a transit but is **not** a natal target in that report.
- `compatibility()` default `chart()` uses the 10-body natal set; other optional bodies are not in its relationships. Profile-order romantic role is fixed on this audit branch.
- Precision root refinement and VOC use purpose-specific windows and body eligibility rather than all possible pairs. A one-hour whole-sky survey is an improvement, **not mathematical proof of exhaustiveness**.
- The historical live deployment at https://mxastro.pages.dev was not browser-certified during this audit; GitHub draft PR and CI are not a release.
- Individual personal narratives, AI output and index weights still require output-specific evidence and complete cross-screen spot checks. A passing test suite alone is not a proof of predictive accuracy.
